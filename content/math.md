## Interests

My main interests in mathematics lie in [number theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_theory) and particularly [arithmetic geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetic_geometry), which studies arithmetic by applying insights  from geometry and topology. 
More specifically, I am interested in Galois representations arising from geometry, arithmetic monodromy, arithmetic statistics, abelian varieties, Drinfeld modules, and rational points. I’m also interested in type theory, mathematical logic, machine learning theory, and formalization of mathematics.
